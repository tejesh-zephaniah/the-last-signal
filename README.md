# 📡 THE LAST SIGNAL

> **What if the station itself was trying to tell you something?**

**The Last Signal** is a first-person sci-fi mystery game built in Godot, where the player wakes up alone inside a remote research and communications station after a catastrophic power failure.

The station is silent.

The emergency beacon is offline.

Something is wrong.

And the deeper you explore, the more the station seems to react to your presence.

---

## 🎮 The Experience

You aren't given a map telling you exactly where to go.

Instead, you explore the station, investigate its rooms, discover objects, listen to radio transmissions, and gradually piece together what happened.

The game is designed around **atmosphere, exploration, mystery, and dynamic events** rather than constant combat.
