# 📡 THE LAST SIGNAL

> **What if the station itself was trying to tell you something?**

**The Last Signal** is a first-person sci-fi mystery game built in **Godot**.

You wake up alone inside a remote research and communications station after a catastrophic power failure.

The station is silent.

The emergency beacon is offline.

Something is wrong.

As you explore the station, you begin discovering clues about what happened. But the deeper you go, the more the station seems to react to your presence.

And then you realize something:

**You are not alone.**

Something is watching you.

---

## 🎮 About the Game

**The Last Signal** focuses on exploration, atmosphere, mystery, and psychological tension rather than constant combat.

You explore the station, investigate rooms, interact with objects, discover clues, listen to radio transmissions, restore systems, and try to understand what happened.

There is no map telling you where to go.

There is no constant stream of objectives telling you what to do next.

You have to explore the station and figure things out for yourself.

But exploration comes with a problem.

### The AI is watching you.

The station contains an AI system that does not simply guide the player.

It actively tries to **distract you**.

It can interfere with the station's systems, disrupt power, trigger alarms, send strange radio transmissions, lock down areas, and create unexpected events.

The closer you get to the truth, the more the AI begins to interfere.
